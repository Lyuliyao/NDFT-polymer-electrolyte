A pair closure can reproduce profiles at a single concentration but cannot adjust its bulk kernel with density. At $\varepsilon_r=7.5$, fitting only $c=0.04$ gives a held-out residual of 0.70 and cation/anion profile errors of 2.5/1.3\%. Its held-out residual rises to 1.30 when $c=0.01$ and 0.02 are added, to 1.55 after adding $c=0.06$, and to 2.19 after adding $c=0.08$. This comparison motivates the density-dependent correlations of the neural functional.

\subsection*{S3.2 Number response}
For $\nb_+=\nb_-=\nb$, write the regular part of $S^{-1}$ in the orthonormal number and charge basis, $\mathbf e_N=(1,1)^{\T}/\sqrt2$ and $\mathbf e_Z=(1,-1)^{\T}/\sqrt2$. In the energy units of section S1,
\[
A_{NN}=\nb^{-1}+\tfrac12(W_{++}+W_{--}+2W_{+-}),\qquad
A_{ZZ}=\nb^{-1}+\tfrac12(W_{++}+W_{--}-2W_{+-}),\qquad
A_{NZ}=\tfrac12(W_{++}-W_{--}).
\]
Inverting Eq.~\ref{eq:S:OZ} gives
\begin{equation}
\Gamma(k)\equiv\frac{2\nb}{S_{NN}(k)}
=1+\frac{\nb}{2}\sum_{\alpha\beta}W_{\alpha\beta}(k)
-\frac{\nb A_{NZ}^2}{A_{ZZ}+8\pi l_B/k^2}.
\label{eq:S:gamma}
\end{equation}
The last term accounts for coupling between number and charge fluctuations. It vanishes as $k\to0$ and contributes 1--4\% of $\Gamma$ at $k_1$ in the reported models. Neglecting that correction yields the MD estimate used in the main text,
\[
\mathbf q^{\T}W(k_1)\mathbf q\simeq\frac{2[\Gamma(k_1)-1]}{\nb},\qquad \mathbf q=(1,1)^{\T}.
\]
Multiplying by $k_BT$ restores energy units.
