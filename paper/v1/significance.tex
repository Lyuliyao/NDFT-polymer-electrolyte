Ion correlations shape the structure and response of polymer electrolytes, but incorporating this molecular information into continuum theories remains challenging. We learn a density functional from molecular simulations with planar ion distributions and use it, without retraining, to predict ionic structure in larger domains and under external fields that vary in two dimensions. The functional preserves the spatial symmetries and thermodynamic consistency of the underlying description while capturing concentration-dependent ion correlations. This ability to retain learned correlations across domain sizes and geometries is essential for connecting molecular simulations to continuum predictions at larger scales.
