Bulk structure and thermodynamic factor. (\textit{A}, \textit{B}) Normalized charge and number structure factors at $\varepsilon_r=7.5$ and $c=0.02$ to 0.08; $c=0.05$ is excluded from training. Points show zero-field MD results averaged over $k$-shells; curves show predictions from Eq.~\ref{eq:OZ}. The small-$k$ charge limit follows from the explicit Coulomb term and the short-ranged excess kernels (Eq.~\ref{eq:SL}). (\textit{C}, \textit{D}) Thermodynamic factor $\Gamma=2\nb/S_{NN}(k_1)$ at $\varepsilon_r=7.5$ and 2. MD uncertainties account for block averages and the autocorrelation time of the $k_1$ mode. Solid curves show the neural functional and dashed curves the pair closure. The dotted line marks the ideal value $\Gamma=1$. Neither quantity is used in training.
