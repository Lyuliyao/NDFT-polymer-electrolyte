**SI Appendix, Fig. S-W2 (Section S8) | Learned kernels at ε_r = 2.**
As Fig. 5, for the ε_r = 2 system (l_B = 29.8 σ). **a**, W_αβ(r) + l_B e_α e_β/r at c = 0.04 (solid) and the bare Coulomb interaction (dotted). **b**, **c**, W_−−(r) and W_++(r) of the neural functional at c = 0.01, 0.04 and 0.08, and of the pair closure (dashed, concentration independent). Contact values are about 3.7 times those at ε_r = 7.5, in proportion to l_B: inside the region the ions do not reach, the learned part largely cancels the Coulomb term. Unlike at ε_r = 7.5, both like-ion channels change with concentration (W_−− at r = σ: −13.1, −8.1, −3.3; W_++: −14.2, −11.4, −7.9 k_BT σ³ at c = 0.01, 0.04, 0.08), while W_+− stays within 10%. Values at 0.5–1.5σ agree between the three seeds to 0.1–0.5 k_BT σ³; contact values (shaded region r < 0.5σ, beyond the wavevectors in the training profiles) differ by up to 15% between seeds.
