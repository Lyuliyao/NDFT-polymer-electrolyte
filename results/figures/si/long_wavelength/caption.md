**SI Appendix, Fig. S-LW (Section S6; final number assigned when the SI is assembled) | The response at the longest wavelengths of the box.**
Formerly main-text Fig. 6; moved to the SI Appendix for PNAS length (2026-09-29).
**a**, Total ion density n_N = n_+ + n_− under a long-wavelength potential (Fourier modes m = 1, 2, k₁ = 2π/L = 0.26 σ⁻¹) at c = 0.02, the run of this family on which the pair closure is worst (a training run). MD with ±2σ band, Poisson–Boltzmann (clipped), the pair closure and the neural functional, all solved from V(z) alone. Relative L₂ errors of n_N: 1.4% (neural functional), 10% (pair closure, its m = 1 amplitude 32% too small), 18% (Poisson–Boltzmann). The response at k₁ is the ideal-gas response divided by the thermodynamic factor Γ = 2n̄/S_NN(k₁), so mean-field theory overshoots by Γ and a functional whose Γ is wrong at a given concentration mis-scales the whole profile.
**b**, **c**, Inverse number structure factor 2n̄/S_NN(k) − 1 at c = 0.01 (b) and c = 0.08 (c): zero-field MD (points; the k₁ and k₂ shells with error bars from blocks × autocorrelation time, circle and triangle) against the two models, evaluated from the second functional derivative at the uniform state. The shaded band marks the m = 1, 2 modes. At k₁ the neural functional gives 0.32 (MD 0.14 ± 0.16) and 7.5 (MD 8.7 ± 0.8); the pair closure gives 0.98 and 7.3, its Γ − 1 being proportional to n̄ by construction.
**d**, Amplitude of the m = 1 mode of n_N on the 25 long-wavelength runs with a number-channel drive (c = 0.02–0.08; open symbols c = 0.05, never trained), predicted by the two models against MD. Predicted over MD, summed per concentration: neural functional 1.05 / 1.14 / 1.14 / 1.05 / 1.08 at c = 0.02 / 0.04 / 0.05 / 0.06 / 0.08; pair closure 0.68 / 0.87 / 0.95 / 0.94 / 1.09. The grey line is the identity.
